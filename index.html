About Me

Hello! I’m DongHyeon Kang, a developer based in South Korea. I enjoy learning, building, and sharing what I discover along the way.

I’m interested in human–AI collaboration and sensing technologies that use novel form factors. I use this space to share my work, document what I learn, and reflect on my experiences.

I have also contributed to research on personalized low-carbon planning and wearable health sensing, including EcoTailor and CapWear.
